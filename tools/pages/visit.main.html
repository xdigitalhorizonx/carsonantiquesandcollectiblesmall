Three ways in

Reno and Sparks come down I-580 from the north. Lake Tahoe comes over the summit on US-50 from the west. Carson Valley comes up US-395 from the south. All three land on North Carson Street.

Before you head out

We're open every day from 10 AM to 6 PM — Saturday and Sunday included — so a weekend drive works as well as a weekday one. Holiday hours aren't posted, so if you're coming on or near a holiday, call (775) 461-3142 first.

Give yourself time. The mall runs over 21,000 square feet across two floors of numbered vendor booths, and there's an upstairs that's easy to miss. One reviewer, Suzy C., spent "a couple of hours just exploring" — that's about the right pace.

Shopping for something big?

If you're hunting a hutch, a dresser or a table, measure the spot it's going and the cargo space you're driving before you leave home. Booths change as vendors sell and restock, so the piece you saw online last month may have found a home — and something better may be sitting in its place.

Once you're here

Look for the yellow sign on North Carson Street and pull into the lot in front of the building. The cashier is at the entrance, and the Treasure Map shows the main floor booth by booth — restrooms included.

Getting here: quick answers

What's the best way to get to the mall from Reno?
Take I-580 south. It's about 31 miles — roughly 40 minutes — to 1831 N Carson St. Route notes are on our Reno trip guide.
Which way do I come from Lake Tahoe?
Take US-50 east over the summit and down into Carson City. Plan on about 45 minutes from Stateline, 50 from Incline Village (NV-28, then US-50) and 55 from South Lake Tahoe.
How far is the mall from Minden and Gardnerville?
16 to 18 miles — about 30 to 35 minutes north on US-395.
How much time should I plan for the mall?
Plan on a couple of hours. It's over 21,000 square feet of vendor booths on two floors, and there's an upstairs.
