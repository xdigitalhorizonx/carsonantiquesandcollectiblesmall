What's in the booths

We make antiquing accessible. This is a multi-vendor mall — numbered booths stocked by different vendors — so every aisle is a different hunt. Six places to start:

  • Collectibles

    A Phillips 66 sign, a California 32 marker, hubcaps and old hand planes. Americana, wall to floor.

  • Home Goods

    Crystal chandeliers, a beaded lace lampshade, pink fringed shades. Home decor, old and newer.

  • China

    Teapots, a blue tea set, cups and saucers on oak shelves. Pattern-matchers, this is your stop.

  • Furniture

    A tall wooden hutch full of dolls, baskets and linens — a single booth. The hutches keep coming.

  • Artwork

    Pencil drawings and old photographs on a booth wall, a vest hung beside them. Art and frames.

  • Currency

    Rows of coins in flip holders in a lit case, under a Coins sign. Collectors, bring your loupe.

Also on the sign out front: Gifts · Home Decor · Art · Toys & More.

The Treasure Map — the main floor, booth by booth

Our printed booth map is called the Treasure Map, and it earns the name. Here's the main floor, redrawn: numbered vendor booths, the display cases, the cashier at the entrance, the restrooms. Pick a corner and take your time. There's an upstairs too — and nobody's timing you.

Questions before you visit

What are your hours?
Every day, 10 AM to 6 PM — Monday through Sunday.
Where is Carson Antiques & Collectibles Mall?
1831 N Carson St, Carson City, NV 89701 — on North Carson Street, under the yellow sign.
Is there parking?
Yes — there's a lot right in front of the building.
Is everything actually antique?
Not everything — and the sign out front says as much: Gifts · Home Decor · Art · Toys & More. This is a multi-vendor mall, so the booths carry antiques, vintage items, collectibles and newer home decor side by side. That mix is the hunt.
How big is the mall?
Over 21,000 square feet on two floors — Northern Nevada's largest antique mall, as the sign out front says.
Is there an upstairs?
Yes. The Treasure Map on this page shows the main floor; there are more booths upstairs.
Are there restrooms?
Yes — they're marked on the Treasure Map.
Can I rent a booth?
That's how we work — vendors rent the numbered booths. Call (775) 461-3142 and ask about current availability.
